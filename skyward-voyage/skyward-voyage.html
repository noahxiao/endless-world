    Steer with your hands

    This viewer doesn't allow webcam access, so hand steering runs when you open the page yourself. Save the page, then open the file in Chrome or Edge and allow the camera.

    1. Hold both hands up in front of the camera, like gripping reins. Hold still for a second while it calibrates.
    2. Raise both hands to climb, lower them to dive.
    3. Lower your left hand (tilt the reins left) to turn left, lower the right to turn right.
    4. Push your hands toward the camera to go faster, pull back to slow down. One hand works too: move it up, down, left or right.

    Here you can steer with arrow keys or WASD, the Reins mode (drag on the view) or a gamepad.
