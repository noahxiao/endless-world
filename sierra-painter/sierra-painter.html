Reading the reference

A backlit alpine lake. Light breaks through a storm at upper centre and falls left onto a granite wall with two ribbon falls; snow peaks sit in the cloud to the right, a dark stand of pines and oaks closes the frame, and a herd of seven deer crosses the shallows toward a green meadow. The painting was read into a 128 × 76 colour map, quantised to the 64 colours below, and every brushstroke samples its colour from it.

What moves once it is finished

  • Storm clouds drift and billow slowly past the peaks.
  • Light shafts breathe, with dust motes turning in the beam.
  • Three waterfalls pour, with spray mist at their feet.
  • The lake ripples, and its reflections and glints shimmer.
  • Pines, oaks and meadow grass sway in gusts of wind.
  • Deer lower their heads to graze; ducks drift; a few birds cross the sky.

The camera never moves. Reference: Albert Bierstadt, Among the Sierra Nevada, California (1868), Smithsonian American Art Museum, public domain. Keys: space plays or pauses, arrows skip 5 s.
